What is the name of the director with the longest career span (measured by years between their first and last directed movie), considering only directors who have directed at least 10 movies?

Find the earliest and latest movie years for each director

Calculate the span between them

Filter for directors with sufficient movies

Order by career span

Use the integrated Query window to the right to execute a Cypher statement to find the answer. Once you have the answer, copy and paste it Cypher statement into the input field below. The field is case insensitive and starting and ending quotes will be stripped.

Your answer
Roman Polanski
To answer this question, you need to:

Match directors to their movies
Filter out movies without year information
Find the minimum and maximum year for each director
Count their total movies
Filter for directors with at least 10 movies
Calculate the career span
Order by span and take the top result

Here's a query that will help you find the answer:

MATCH (d:Director)-[:DIRECTED]->(m:Movie)
WHERE m.year IS NOT NULL
WITH d, min(m.year) as firstYear, max(m.year) as lastYear, COUNT(m) as movieCount
WHERE movieCount >= 10
WITH d, lastYear - firstYear as careerSpan, movieCount
RETURN d.name, careerSpan, movieCount
ORDER BY careerSpan DESC
LIMIT 1

Your answer

The answer is "Zeppo Marx". His movies have an average user rating of 4.04 from 53 total RATED relationships, making him the highest-rated Marx brother based on user ratings in the database.

To find this answer, we need to run a Cypher statement that follows user RATED relationships to movies:

MATCH (a:Actor)-[:ACTED_IN]->(m:Movie)<-[r:RATED]-(u:User)

Match actors to movies to genres

Count total movies per actor

Order by genre count first, then by movie count for ties
